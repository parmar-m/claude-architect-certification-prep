# CCA-P Scenarios (placeholder)

**The official CCA-P exam guide (v1.0, July 2026) defines no scenario bank.** Unlike CCA-F (4 scenarios drawn from a bank of 6), the CCA-P exam is structured purely as **63 items across 7 weighted domains**; see the [exam guide](../../official-exam-guides/cca-p-exam-guide.pdf), Sec. 5–6.

This folder exists as a placeholder in case a future guide revision introduces scenarios. Until then, study by domain:

[D1 Solution Design](../domains/d1-solution-design.md) · [D2 Models, Prompting & Context](../domains/d2-models-prompting-context.md) · [D3 Integration](../domains/d3-integration.md) · [D4 Evaluation](../domains/d4-evaluation-testing-optimization.md) · [D5 Governance](../domains/d5-governance-safety-risk.md) · [D6 Stakeholders & Lifecycle](../domains/d6-stakeholder-lifecycle.md) · [D7 Dev Productivity](../domains/d7-dev-productivity-enablement.md)

> Note: CCA-P *questions* are still scenario-flavored (short production vignettes, see the 3 samples in the guide), but there is no fixed published scenario bank to prepare against.
